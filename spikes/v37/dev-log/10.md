# dev10 — a differently worded credit check (REJECTED, reverted)

**Change tried:** when the whole-answer check says correct, a second call asks whether any part (a reason,
a mechanism, an example) is false ("clean" / "flawed"); a flawed answer keeps its coverage but writes no
credit before the question. In the probe it rejected 2 of the 3 over-credits and kept 13/16 positives.

## Result and decision

* P looked better: D 75.0% coarse, commit accuracy 85.7%, false mastery 1/58, harmful writes 5%.
* But **in the pipeline the credit check called 9 of 16 positive answers "flawed"** (probe: 3/16),
  including both correct canonical answers: **C1 and C3 fail in all three runs** (asked, no credit), so
  canonical fell to 3/5. The P gain came from asking more: **follow-up 65%, over the G7 ceiling (≤ 60%)**.
* **Rejected and reverted.** The prompt-set hash is back to dev09's
  (`bd7847e75be8b28a36fb73dd6661be4f003cf2c4464b72fcce741b14c4ec583e`); the spike tests pass on the
  reverted code. Latency in this run was again inflated by machine load (a cold call of 11.9 s).
