# dev14 — omitted negation; reason clauses canonicalized; link check removed (candidate)

**Changes:**
* **V3 split.** V3a: an affirmative segment credited with a *negated* claim restates what the source denies
  ("it is encrypted" for "not automatically encrypted"). It is never credit, is not rescued by the claim
  family and is not overridden by agreement; it feeds the unchanged judge's own omitted-negation rule
  (V35's `unexpressedNegation`, hard-wired to false in the spike adapter until now). Other polarity
  mismatches stay heuristic.
* **Reason clauses canonicalized.** A clause opening with a reason marker that is not folded into a credited
  conclusion and is unmatched, or restates a negated claim, is an unsettled reason (weak reasoning, asked).
  The row's claim choice for such a clause varied by run; either way the reasoning is what is in doubt.
* **The link check removed** (runner and harness): it measured position, not reasoning.
* Rejected after measurement (all on the same seven real-model runs, re-scored):
  * claim-family anchoring for secondary families (lexical): coarse 74.8 → 72.9%, novel vocabulary 67.6 →
    64.8% (below G3), and the instability only moved to other answers;
  * a per-claim coverage call (states / omits): perfectly stable and order-free, but "states" for every
    secondary idea, including all six gold "mostly" answers — deterministic by discarding the distinction;
  * a claim-selection call: stable (17/18) but lists the secondary claim for nearly every answer.
* Weak reasoning vs misconception, self-consistency pattern (a credited claim contradicted only downstream of
  a reason): 4 gold misconceptions, 2 weak reasoning — no structural separation; gd-071 stays.
* Novel vocabulary by category: 6 misses are correct paraphrases the check calls "vague" (no structural
  separation from genuinely vague answers: identical row features for 18 positive and 48 weak instances),
  4 are misconceptions V9 cannot ground (relaxing V9 would add 5 false misconceptions). No change.
