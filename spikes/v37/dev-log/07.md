# dev07 — the locator chooses the likely mistake

**Change:** the locator sees the answer key's likely mistakes and picks one (or none) after stating what
the textbook says instead; a chosen mistake fixes the segment's misconception id and its claim (the key
ties each mistake to one claim). The `instead` text and the mistake id are recorded on the locate item.

## Result

* **Canonical 5/5 in all three runs**, identical decisions. C5: the locator picks `m2` ("JWTs are always
  encrypted.") and the claim "not automatically encrypted", with `instead` = "JWTs are signed claim
  containers, not automatically encrypted." in every run.
* P: D 63.7% (dev06 66.2%). **This is run-to-run noise, not the change:** 14 of 80 D decisions differ
  from dev06, including answers whose prompts are byte-identical (X-04, X-09, gd-062: different row
  labels). Greedy decoding is not deterministic on this runtime. A single P run carries about ±3 cases.
* **Latency: warm p95 11.3 s, max 14.4 s**, from machine load, not the change: the same reading calls ran
  2× slower during answers 40–70 (mobileassetd, ESET scanning, Chrome, node active). Re-measured later.

## Failure analysis (D errors, 29)

* 8 weak answers the check calls "vague" still reach "mostly": a full credit V7 had already made
  tentative escaped D's dispute (it downgraded only firm credit). Fixed in dev08.
* 3 misconception premises linked by "so"/"because" to a credited conclusion are folded as weak
  reasoning (gd-063 gd-065 gd-085). The locator's `kind` would separate them in this run, but it flips
  between runs (dev06: the same misconceptions were "wrongReason"), so it stays out of the decision.
* 3 positive paraphrases the check calls vague; 4 answers where the check itself is wrong.
