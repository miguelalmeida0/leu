# dev09 — the cap only on disputed credit; reasons both readers accept are settled

**Changes (D only):**
* The one-partial-credit cap applies only when the check disputes the answer. Before, it also removed
  credits V3/V7 had made tentative on answers the check called correct (gd-035: "Because of the
  signature" lost its credit and became an unsupported reason).
* A reason folded into its conclusion is settled (`ReasonReading.supported`) when both readers take it
  to be true: the row reads it as correct and the check calls the answer correct (gd-003). One reader
  alone leaves it unsettled, and the judge asks.

## Result

* **D 73.8% coarse**, paraphrase 68%, novel vocabulary 66%, WR 55% / 65%, false mastery 3/58, commit
  accuracy 81%, harmful writes 10%. Canonical 5/5 in all three runs.
* **Latency invalid again (machine load):** warm p50 10.8 s, p95 17.3 s, cold ~9 s for calls identical in
  number and shape to dev06 (p50 3.8 s). Right after the run, a single whole-answer check took 0.46–0.55 s,
  as in the first probes, while `textunderstandingd` and `mediaanalysisd` (Apple Intelligence background
  daemons) were busy: system work sharing the on-device model slows the spike's calls 2–3×.
