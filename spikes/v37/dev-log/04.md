# dev04 — the locator

**Probes first (P, open):** a leading `overall` field inside the reading *degrades* the verdict (37/80 in
context vs 58/80 standalone); the rows still call most weak answers correct. A **locator** asked only
when the check finds the answer wrong places the error plausibly and in ~0.8 s; its `kind` separated all
8 weak-reasoning answers as `wrongReason` in the probe.

**Change:** the reading is now two model calls: rows (no gist) in parallel with check → locator (only
when the check says mistaken/flawedReason; it may answer `none`). The locator's placement is merged into
the recorded reading; the raw rows are kept in `rows`. D: a located wrong reason after a credited
statement becomes that statement's wrong reason.

## Failure analysis

* **The locator's `kind` is unreliable in the pipeline:** it called 19 misconceptions `wrongReason`, so
  the attach rule turned misconceptions into weak reasoning (misconception recall 7/28). Rejected.
* **Partial credit on two core claims sums to "mostly"** (0.75 + 0.5 of 2.5): vague answers land positive.
* Canonical: the check + locator is stable across runs (C2 seg 1, C4 seg 2, C5 seg 2), but C2's row
  polarity is `affirmed`, C4's model links are junk, C5's located claim is the purpose claim.
