# dev03 — V37 composition, claim families, and the whole-answer check gating D

**Changes (general mechanisms, in the spike path only):**
* Segmenter: conclusion markers (`so`, `therefore`, `thus`, `hence`, `consequently`, `which means`,
  `that's why`, `as a result`) split like reason markers; `as a result` fixed (it introduces a result,
  not a reason). Reason → conclusion links come from the markers' direction; C and D use only these.
* Risk 3: a credited premise linked to a wrong conclusion loses its credit (check `P1`).
* Risk 1: claim families (a supporting claim joins the core claim it shares most content stems with,
  else the definition); coverage and credit are recorded on the family's core claim (`F1`).
* Risk 2: partial entailment is never firm credit (`V11`).
* D: the whole-answer check must agree before a write is firm; disputed credit is partial at most.
* Inputs re-exported (11 P answers and C5 gained a split; C5 is now "JWTs are signed" / "so nobody can
  read them.").
