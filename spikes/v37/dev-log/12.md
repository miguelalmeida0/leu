# dev12 — a wrong reason is asked about, never committed (the frozen configuration)

**Change (adapter, every configuration):** a reason given for a credited conclusion is emitted as an
unsettled reason (unless both readers take it to be true), so the judge states weak reasoning and asks
where it breaks, at the conclusion claim's family. Before, a confirmed wrong reason was committed as weak
reasoning through the judge's own unsupported-reason rule. This is the canonical C4 behaviour ("no
mastery, mechanism-targeted probe"), and committed, it wrote weak reasoning over three misconceptions.

**Method:** the change is in the harness only, so the three dev11 real-model runs were re-scored under it
(`readings/*-dev12-*` are copies of dev11's): the same model output, a different rule, no decoding noise.
