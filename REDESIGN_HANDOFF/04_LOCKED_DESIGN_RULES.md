# LEU — LOCKED DESIGN RULES

These are not suggestions.

## Typography

**Never use handwritten / script / scribble typography.**

This includes:

- fake handwritten annotations;
- script motivational copy;
- marker/scribble labels;
- decorative "human" notes;
- handwriting as a visual shortcut for warmth.

Use deliberate product typography: strong sans, editorial serif where justified, labels and numerals with real hierarchy.

## Copy

- No generic inspirational taglines.
- No fake wellness language.
- No "you've got this" filler.
- No AI assistant voice unless the interaction genuinely requires it.
- Avoid repeatedly saying "evidence" in product copy.
- Use actual Leu material and actual source concepts in demos: React Notes, keys/identity, state snapshot, Event Loop, HTTP caching, SQL indexes, transactions, etc.

## No AI slop

Avoid recognizable generic 2024–2026 AI-design habits:

- purple as a default just because "AI";
- generic glassmorphism;
- floating glowing orbs;
- meaningless auroras;
- fake gradients that carry no information;
- oversized neon glow everywhere;
- endless soft rounded cards;
- arbitrary blob backgrounds;
- sparkle icons to mean intelligence;
- chatbot boxes as the universal interaction;
- fake scientific scores;
- stock landscape / playlist imagery unrelated to the user's documents;
- "dashboard" presentation of every capability.

Strong color and glow can be used when art-directed and purposeful. The issue is genericness, not a ban on color.

## Layout

- Fewer containers.
- Fewer permanent CTAs.
- Strong reading width and hierarchy.
- Avoid narrow SaaS center-column layouts.
- Full-card click affordance when an entire object is actionable.
- Keep the primary navigation simple.
- Do not create a second global nav inside Study or Reader.
- Do not turn Connections into a generic graph.

## Interaction

- iPhone-native physicality: touch, haptics, motion, long-press, audio, interruption/resume.
- Animations must explain state, continuity or causality rather than decorate.
- No loaders like "preparing your screen".
- Maintain at least 44pt effective touch targets.
- Preserve Dynamic Type and VoiceOver order.

## Sequence/reconstruction guidance

For ordering tasks such as HTTP Caching:

- confirmed-correct position → **green check inside its circle/step indicator**;
- unresolved/incorrect → neutral;
- progressive guidance only;
- do not reveal the full answer;
- no punitive red-heavy treatment.

## Reading

Books remain central.
A redesign that makes Study visually dominant at the cost of reading is a regression.

## Intelligence

Intelligence should appear as better explanations, questions, connections and next actions—not as a permanent "AI" brand layer.
